# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-29): the first public release

- **Four campaigns, 32 levels:** the Horde (*Blood and Thunder*), the Alliance (*Shadows over Stormwind*), the Sentinels (*Wrath of the Wild*, at 30 stars) and the Forsaken (*The Dark Lady's Will*, at 55 stars). Each has eight levels in real zones, from a short first level to a 15-wave siege against its big boss, and a story told in briefings, battle cries, boss lines and victory lines.
- **Twenty towers**, five per campaign by role (ranged, area, control, siege and the campaign's signature tower), each with two specializations: chain lightning, War Stomp, Blizzard, moonglaives, spreading plague, corpse explosions, curses and more. **Four heroes**, each with an ability every three minutes: Thrall's Bloodlust, Bolvar's Divine Shield, Tyrande's Starfall and Sylvanas's Wailing Arrow.
- **Creeps and bosses with abilities:** stealth (only some towers reveal it), healers, regeneration, spiders that split when they die, summons, blinks, stomps, shields and enrages.
- **Progress:** three stars per level, Heroic mode, and Warmarks to spend in the Armory: five tiers of research per tower, plus a War Chest (more starting gold) and a Militia (more lives) that help in every campaign.
- **Easy in and out:** closing the window pauses the game, and the whole state survives a `/reload`, mid-wave included. Played with the mouse alone: no keybinds. Normal and double speed, auto waves, window sizes from 0.6 to 1.6, and an entry in the minimap's addon menu.
- Drawn entirely with the game's own art; the addon ships no images.

### Before 1.0

Versions 0.1 to 0.4.8 (2026-10-17 to 2026-10-23) were private test rounds, played on WoW: Forever's beta client: the first playable level, then three zones, then the four campaigns, followed by balance passes from real play on a fresh profile. Every level was tuned against two simulated players, a newcomer and a good player, so that Normal leaves a newcomer some room and three stars take good play.
